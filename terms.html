8. Fees and payments

There are three ways to sell gear, and the seller chooses when they create the listing.

Pay per listing. A listing fee of NZD $1.99 per listing, or NZD $5 for a pack of five, and no commission. When the item sells the seller receives the full listing price, and a card processing fee of 2.9% plus 30 cents is added to the buyer's payment to cover the cost of the card payment.

Success fee. No listing fee. When the item sells, a commission of 4.99% of the sale price (minimum NZD $0.99, capped at NZD $49) is deducted from the seller's payout, together with the card processing fee of 2.9% plus 30 cents. The buyer pays the price shown on the listing.

Business storefront. Registered business sellers pay no listing fees and may list without limit. Commission is charged on the same terms as the success fee above: 4.99% of the sale price (minimum NZD $0.99, capped at NZD $49), deducted from the seller's payout together with the card processing fee of 2.9% plus 30 cents.

Private sales. A seller may instead pay NZD $1.99 to list an item and arrange payment directly with the buyer. No commission is charged and no money passes through The Tack Market, so buyer protection does not apply and the parties carry the risk of the transaction themselves.

Horses and ponies. Listing an animal costs NZD $25. A member's first animal is free to list until 31 December 2026. Relisting an animal you have already listed with us is not charged again. No commission is charged on the sale of an animal and no money passes through The Tack Market.

Card payments are processed by Stripe and are subject to Stripe's own terms. Fees may be varied or waived during promotional periods. Disputes are handled per Stripe's policies and our support channel.
